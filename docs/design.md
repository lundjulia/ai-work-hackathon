# Design baseline (Figma: "Household Budget App – CodeNode Prototype")

Source: https://www.figma.com/proto/SnnKbGgd6HzSh8gmyN8jA8/ (4 frames). Overview: [design/overview.png](design/overview.png). Mascot: [design/penny.png](design/penny.png). Follow this; it supersedes earlier free-form styling.

## Look
- Font: geometric sans, bold tight headings (use **Plus Jakarta Sans** via `next/font/google`).
- Colors: forest green `#1F4A39` (primary, hero cards, dark tip cards, active nav), mid green `#4F8A63`, sage `#A8D5AF` (progress fill on dark, accent text), pale green `#DCEBDD` (chips/"On track" badge, progress track), mustard `#F2C24B` (mascot, accent), app bg warm off-white `#F2F2EE`, cards white `#FFFFFF`, light grey `#DADAD5`, text near-black `#14231C`, muted `#6B746E`.
- Cards: white, radius ~20px, no borders, no heavy shadows, generous padding. Big kr numbers (e.g. "1,240 kr" with small muted "of 1,800 kr" next to it).
- Bottom nav: floating white pill bar, 4 tabs (Home, Budget, Goals, Family) with active tab in pale-green pill + forest icon. We use 5: Home `/`, Budget `/budget`, Meals `/meals`, Penny `/advisor`, Goals `/goals`.
- Mascot **Penny**: mustard-yellow scalloped/cloud-shaped round body, simple black face (one dot eye + one winking eye, small smile), thin black crossed arms over the belly, two thin straight legs (black outline, white fill). Openly an AI: "Hi, I'm Penny. I'm an AI, and I'm on your side." White speech bubble with tail.
